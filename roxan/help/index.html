Help

Getting started,and getting unstuck.

Three steps to your first conversation, then the questions people ask most.

Getting started

  • Open Roxan. Go to roxan.oplocloud.com/chat.
  • Enter your invite code when Roxan asks. It is kept on this device.
  • Say something. Roxan keeps the conversation going from here.

Things to try

  • “Remind me to call the dentist tomorrow at 9.”
  • “Remember that I prefer metric units.”
  • Attach a document and ask for the three decisions it needs.
  • Open Projects, make one, and give it instructions.

What Roxan cannot do yet

Browse the web, read your email or calendar, act in other apps, speak, or remind you when it is not open. These are listed as Next on the product page. If you ask, Roxan will say so rather than pretend.

Keyboard

  • Focus the message box/

Questions

Where is my history kept?

On this device, in this browser. It is not stored on Oplo’s servers. Download a copy from Settings.

How do I delete something Roxan remembers?

Open Memory, find it, and choose Forget. It is removed immediately and is not used in any later reply. To delete everything, use Settings.

Why did a reminder not fire?

Reminders fire only while Roxan is open in a browser tab. If one came due while you were away, Roxan shows it as missed when you return. Check that notifications are allowed for this site.

Does Roxan train on my conversations?

Roxan does not train models. Your messages are sent to a language-model provider to be answered; the Security page lists exactly what is sent.

Roxan said it cannot do something I expected.

That is intended. Roxan tells you what it cannot do instead of pretending. See what is coming on the product page.
