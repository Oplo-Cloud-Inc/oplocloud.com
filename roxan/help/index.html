Open Roxan

Getting started,and getting unstuck.

Three steps to your first conversation, then the questions people ask most.

Getting started

  • Open Roxan. Go to roxan.oplocloud.com/chat.
  • Enter your invite code when Roxan asks. It is kept on this device.
  • Say something. Roxan keeps the conversation going from here.

Things to try

  • “Remind me to call the dentist tomorrow at 9.”
  • “Remember that Priya is my manager.” Roxan keeps the note and shows you, with an Undo. See the web it builds in Memory.
  • “Make a specialty for negotiating a return.”
  • “Book a table for two on Friday at 7.” Roxan writes it up as a Handoff for you to save.
  • Attach a document and ask for the three decisions it needs.
  • Open Projects, make one, and give it instructions.

What Roxan cannot do yet

Use a web browser, sign in to a website, read your email or calendar, act in other apps, speak, or remind you when it is not open. A Handoff is a task written down for later, not one that is run, and nothing from the Vault is filled into any website yet. These are listed as Next on the product page. If you ask, Roxan will say so rather than pretend.

Questions

Where is my history kept?

On this device, in this browser. It is not stored on Oplo’s servers. Download a copy from Settings.

Does Roxan keep notes without asking me?

Yes, while learning is on, which it is by default. When you tell Roxan something lasting, it keeps a short note on this device and shows you in the conversation, with an Undo. It never keeps one straight away from a message with an attachment or from anything that looks like a password, a card or a medical detail; those wait for your yes. Turn learning off in Settings and it only suggests.

How do I delete something Roxan remembers?

Press Undo on the note right after it is kept, or open Memory, find it, and choose Forget. It is removed immediately and is not used in any later reply, or suggested again. To delete everything, use Settings.

What is a Handoff?

A task that needs a web browser, such as paying a bill or booking a table. Ask Roxan and it proposes one for you to read, change and save; every Handoff is listed under Handoff in the sidebar. Roxan cannot use a browser yet, so a saved Handoff waits and nothing runs. You mark it done when you have done it yourself.

What is a specialty?

Steps for a task you want done the same way every time. Roxan follows the ones that fit what you ask. Write one under Specialties, ask Roxan to make one, or let it set one up while learning is on. A specialty is only words and never acts on its own.

Who can open my Vault?

Only you, with your passphrase. Items are sealed in your browser before they are saved, and the passphrase and key never leave it, so Roxan, its model and Oplo cannot read them. There is no reset: if you forget the passphrase the items cannot be recovered, and you can delete the Vault and make a new one. Do not type a card number into a message; keep it in the Vault.

Why did a reminder not fire?

Reminders fire only while Roxan is open in a browser tab. If one came due while you were away, Roxan shows it as missed when you return. Check that notifications are allowed for this site.

Does Roxan train on my conversations?

Roxan does not train models. Your messages are sent to a language-model provider to be answered; the Security page lists exactly what is sent.

Roxan said it cannot do something I expected.

That is intended. Roxan tells you what it cannot do instead of pretending. See what is coming on the product page.
