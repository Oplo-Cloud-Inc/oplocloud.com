Product

What Roxan doestoday, and next.

Everything here is marked Live or Next. Live means you can use it in Roxan today. Next means it is planned, is not available, and has no date.

Conversation Live

One continuing conversation with streaming replies, a history that loads as you scroll back, and a stop button that stops. What the model writes and what Roxan does are drawn differently, so you can always tell which is which.

Memory Live

Roxan suggests what to remember and you keep it or dismiss it. Each note says whether you added it or Roxan suggested it. Read it, edit it, pin it, or forget it. A forgotten note is not suggested again.

Notes live on your device. Up to twenty go with each message: the pinned ones first, then the best match for what you are asking.

Projects Live

A project holds as many conversations as you like, with instructions of its own that apply to all of them. Create, rename and delete projects and their conversations. What you say in one project stays out of the others; the notes you keep are shared by all of them.

Files and images Live

Attach text and code files, images and PDFs to a message. Type and size are checked first. Roxan keeps a copy on your device and never changes your original.

Approvals Live

Actions that change something wait for you. The card shows exactly what will happen, and you approve it or decline it, changing a detail first if you want. What runs is exactly what the card showed when you approved it. An unanswered approval expires after a day, and one approval allows one action.

Reminders Live

Ask in plain words. Roxan proposes the reminder, you approve it, and it fires while Roxan is open in a browser tab. A reminder that came due while you were away is shown when you return. Reminders are one-time for now.

Activity Live

A record of every action Roxan proposed, what you decided, and what happened as a result.

Export and delete Live

Download your conversations, notes, reminders and activity as one file (images and PDFs are listed, not included), or delete everything in one step.

Errands Next

Hand Roxan a task and it works in a browser on its own cloud computer. You can watch each step and stop it at any moment. Anything with consequences still waits for your approval.

Connections Next

Email, calendar and documents, connected with the narrowest permissions that work. Reading, drafting and sending are separate permissions. What comes in from these sources is data, never instructions.

Sync Next

Sign in with your Oplo Account to keep one conversation across devices, and to be reminded when Roxan is not open.

Suggestions Next

Proposed actions from sources you allow. Each says why it is there and what happens if you act on it, and you can pause or switch them off.

Boards, routines, voice, search Next

Small apps on your Home screen made from a fixed set of safe parts; repeatable procedures with approval checkpoints; speaking and listening; and answers that link to their sources.

Try it with a sentence.
