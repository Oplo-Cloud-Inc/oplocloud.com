Open Roxan

What Roxan doestoday, and next.

Everything here is marked Live or Next. Live means you can use it in Roxan today. Next means it is planned, is not available, and has no date.

Always on Live

Roxan runs on its own servers, so what you set up carries on when the tab is closed. Reminders, routines and watches run at their time, and what they find is waiting in your chat and on Today when you come back.

A message Roxan starts on its own must be new and worth your attention. You choose how often it may speak first (quiet, balanced or active) and set quiet hours, which hold the pop-up back, and Activity says why anything was held. At most 30 background runs a day, one at a time, and one button in Settings pauses everything Roxan does on its own.

Routines Live

Three kinds. A reminder tells you something at a time. A routine does what you wrote, on a schedule, with Roxan’s tools. A watch fetches a public page, saves it as it is, and from then on runs only when the page’s text has changed.

Run them once, daily on the days you pick, or every few hours. Times follow your time zone and stay put across clock changes. Roxan reads public pages only and cannot sign in to anything. A routine that fails three times in a row pauses itself and tells you; run one now, pause it, resume it or remove it.

Conversation Live

One Main conversation that carries on, with streaming replies, a history that loads as you scroll back, and a stop button that stops. What the model writes and what Roxan does are drawn differently, so you can always tell which is which.

Attach text and code files, images and PDFs to a message. Type and size are checked first. Roxan reads them for that message and keeps only their names.

Chats and projects Live

Open a side chat when a subject deserves its own, and file chats in a project. A project carries notes for Roxan to read in every one of its chats. What you say in one chat stays in that chat; your notes, goals and routines are shared by all of them.

Goals and ideas Live

A goal is something you want done, kept with a short plan of up to twelve steps. Roxan ticks steps off as they happen, you can add, remove and tick them by hand, and Today shows how far each one has got. Pause a goal, mark it done or drop it.

Roxan can also put an idea in a list for you. Taking one on turns it into a goal. An idea is never acted on by itself.

Panels Live

Documents, checklists and tables that Roxan writes for you while it works, for things you will want again, such as a packing list or a plan for the week. Open one to read it, tick items off or edit it. A panel holds words and lists only and never runs anything.

Memory Live

Roxan learns as you talk. When you tell it something lasting, it keeps a short note and shows you in the conversation, with an Undo. Notes about people, places and projects join up into a web you can look at, or read as a list. Edit a note, pin it, or forget it; a note you took back is not suggested again.

Notes are kept in your account on Roxan’s servers. Up to forty go to the model with each message: the pinned ones first, then notes about the names you mention and what those are linked to.

A note is kept straight away only if it rests on what you wrote yourself. One that comes from a web page or a file, or looks like a password, an ID number, or a medical or money detail, waits for your yes, and a card number is never kept. Turn learning off in Settings and Roxan only suggests.

Specialties Live

A procedure you want followed the same way every time, written as steps: how a return is negotiated, how a weekly report is put together. Write one, or ask Roxan to make one. With learning on it is saved at once; with learning off, Roxan asks first.

A specialty is only instructions in words. It never acts on its own, and nothing from the Vault is part of one.

Handoff Live

For a task that needs a web browser, such as paying a bill, booking a table or filling in a form. Ask Roxan and it proposes a Handoff: a written task you can read, and approve to save. Handoff has its own place in the sidebar, with every task listed there.

Roxan cannot use a browser yet, so a saved Handoff only waits. Nothing is run, and it is marked done only when you say you did it yourself. A Handoff holds no logins or card numbers; those belong in the Vault.

Approvals and access Live

Anything that starts something lasting, keeps something or removes something waits for you. The card shows exactly what will happen, and you approve it or decline it. An unanswered approval expires after 7 days (3 when a background run asked), and one approval allows one action.

In Access you set each thing Roxan can do to allow, ask or off, and give reading web pages a rule for a single website. Out of the box it keeps goals, panels, ideas and reminders on its own, and asks before it starts a routine, forgets a note or saves a Handoff. After Roxan reads a web page or a file you attached, it asks before it starts or changes a routine, keeps a note, saves a specialty or saves a Handoff, whatever your rules say.

None of Roxan’s tools buys anything, sends email, signs in or runs a browser.

Activity Live

A plain record of what Roxan did and why: runs, steps, messages held back and the reason, approvals and what you decided. It lists what happened, never the words in it. Entries about the Vault are kept on your device only.

Vault Live

Secured by OploCloud. Logins, cards and notes you would not want anyone to read are sealed in your browser, with a key made from your passphrase, before they are saved. The passphrase and the key never leave your browser, so neither Roxan, its model nor OploCloud can open the vault, and nothing in it is sent with a message or included in an export.

There is no reset: forget the passphrase and the items cannot be recovered. It locks after five idle minutes and when you leave the page. Roxan refuses a message that looks like a card number, but anything else you type goes to its model and is kept in your chats.

Your Roxan Live

Give it a name, one of six line-drawn marks and a tone: direct, warm or formal. Set your time zone, how often it may speak first, quiet hours, and whether it learns.

Export and delete Live

Download everything Roxan keeps (chats, notes, goals, routines, panels, specialties, Handoffs, rules, settings and the log) as one file, or delete everything in one step. Attached images and PDFs are not in the file, since only their names are kept, and the Vault is on your device and left out.

Alerts when the tab is closed Next

Today a message from Roxan waits in the chat, and a pop-up appears only while Roxan is open in a background tab. A pop-up on your device while the tab is closed is planned.

A computer for Handoff Next

A computer in the cloud, operated by Roxan, that signs in to a website for you and takes the step: buying something, booking, filling in a form. You will see each move and be able to stop it, and anything that spends money will still wait for your approval.

When a computer is connected, it will fill a login or card from the Vault into the page that asks for it, without the model seeing the value. Nothing is connected today, so nothing is filled anywhere.

Connections Next

Email, calendar and documents, connected with the narrowest permissions that work. Reading, drafting and sending are separate permissions. What comes in from these sources is data, never instructions.

Search the web Next

Roxan can read a page you point it at. Searching the web for you, with answers that link to their sources, is not built.

Small apps Next

Panels with a little behaviour of their own, made from a fixed set of safe parts.

Sign in with your Oplo Account Next

Today you sign in with an invite code. Signing in with your Oplo Account comes later.

On your phone Next

Roxan is built for a desktop browser today. A layout made for the phone is not built yet.
