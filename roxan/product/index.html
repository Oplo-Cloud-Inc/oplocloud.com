Product

What Roxan doestoday, and next.

Everything here is marked Live or Next. Live means you can use it in Roxan today. Next means it is planned, is not available, and has no date.

Conversation Live

One continuing conversation with streaming replies, a history that loads as you scroll back, and a stop button that stops. What the model writes and what Roxan does are drawn differently, so you can always tell which is which.

Handoff Live

For a task that needs a web browser, such as paying a bill, booking a table or filling in a form. Ask Roxan and it proposes a Handoff: a written task you can read, change and save. Handoff has its own place in the sidebar, with every task listed there.

Roxan cannot use a browser yet, so a saved Handoff only waits. Nothing is run, and it is marked done only when you say you did it yourself. A Handoff holds no logins or card numbers; those belong in the Vault.

Memory Live

Roxan learns as you talk. When you tell it something lasting, it keeps a short note in the background and shows you in the conversation, with an Undo. Notes about people, places and projects join up into a web you can look at, or read as a list. Edit a note, pin it, or forget it; a forgotten or undone note is not suggested again.

Notes live on your device. Up to twenty go with each message: the pinned ones first, then notes about the names you mention and what those are linked to. The reply says how many it was shown.

Nothing is kept straight away from a file or image you attach, or from anything that looks like a password, a card or a medical detail; those wait for your yes. Turn learning off in Settings and Roxan only suggests.

Specialties Live

A procedure you want followed the same way every time, written as steps: how a return is negotiated, how a weekly report is put together. Write one, ask Roxan to make one, or, with learning on, let Roxan set one up when it sees you do the same task the same way. It is shown with an Undo.

A specialty is only instructions in words. It never acts on its own, and nothing from the Vault is part of one.

Vault Live

Secured by OploCloud. Logins, cards and notes you would not want anyone to read are sealed in your browser, with a key made from your passphrase, before they are saved. The passphrase and the key never leave your browser, so neither Roxan, its model nor OploCloud can open the vault, and nothing in it is sent with a message or included in an export.

There is no reset: forget the passphrase and the items cannot be recovered. It locks after five idle minutes and when you leave the page. Roxan warns you before a message that looks like a card number is sent, because anything typed into a message does go to the model.

Projects Live

A project holds as many conversations as you like, with instructions of its own that apply to all of them. Create, rename and delete projects and their conversations. What you say in one project stays out of the others; the notes you keep are shared by all of them.

Files and images Live

Attach text and code files, images and PDFs to a message. Type and size are checked first. Roxan keeps a copy on your device and never changes your original.

Approvals Live

Actions that change something wait for you. The card shows exactly what will happen, and you approve it or decline it, changing a detail first if you want. What runs is exactly what the card showed when you approved it. An unanswered approval expires after a day, and one approval allows one action. The exception is a short note or specialty learned while learning is on: it is kept at once and shown with an Undo, and it only ever writes down words.

Reminders Live

Ask in plain words. Roxan proposes the reminder, you approve it, and it fires while Roxan is open in a browser tab. A reminder that came due while you were away is shown when you return. Reminders are one-time for now.

Activity Live

A record of every action Roxan proposed, what you decided, and what happened as a result.

Export and delete Live

Download your conversations, notes, specialties, Handoffs, reminders and activity as one file (images and PDFs are listed, not included, and the Vault stays sealed and is left out), or delete everything in one step.

A computer for Handoff Next

A computer in the cloud, operated by Roxan, that signs in to a website for you and takes the step: buying something, booking, filling in a form. You will see each move and be able to stop it, and anything that spends money will still wait for your approval.

When a computer is connected, it will fill a login or card from the Vault into the page that asks for it, without the model seeing the value. Nothing is connected today, so nothing is filled anywhere. A button for the Handoffs you repeat comes after that.

Connections Next

Email, calendar and documents, connected with the narrowest permissions that work. Reading, drafting and sending are separate permissions. What comes in from these sources is data, never instructions.

Sync Next

Sign in with your Oplo Account to keep one conversation across devices, and to be reminded when Roxan is not open.

Suggestions Next

Proposed actions from sources you allow. Each says why it is there and what happens if you act on it, and you can pause or switch them off.

Boards, voice, search Next

Small apps on your Home screen made from a fixed set of safe parts; speaking and listening; and answers that link to their sources.
