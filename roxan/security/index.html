Open Roxan

Security

Built to bechecked.

Plain statements about what Roxan stores, what it sends, how it is stopped from acting on its own, and what we are not claiming.

What stays on your device

Your conversation, memory, projects, reminders and the files you attach are stored in this browser. They are not stored on Oplo’s servers. If you clear this browser’s site data, they are gone; export first to keep a copy.

What leaves it

To write a reply, Roxan sends the recent conversation, the few memories chosen for that message, the project’s instructions, and any attachments in recent messages to a language-model provider through Roxan’s gateway.

The gateway checks your invite code, applies limits, and passes the request on. It does not store or log the content of messages. The provider’s own handling of that content is governed by its terms, which Roxan cannot change.

How actions are controlled

Roxan can only use the actions it has been given, and each action has a fixed shape that is checked before anything happens. Text written by the model never counts as permission.

  • Actions that change something wait for your approval, showing exactly what will happen.
  • You can change a detail on a card before you approve it; what runs is exactly what the card showed when you pressed Approve. An unanswered approval expires after a day, and one approval allows one action.
  • If anything is missing, unclear or expired, the action does not run.
  • Roxan says an action is done only after it has been confirmed.

Untrusted content

Files you attach, pages and other content Roxan reads are treated as data. They cannot change Roxan’s rules or grant it permission. Roxan can propose a memory, but it is only kept if you keep it. Replies are shown as text and links; nothing in a reply is executed.

Your controls

Read, edit or delete any memory. Delete a project with its conversation and attachments. Export your data (images and PDFs are listed, not included), or delete everything in one step. Deleting removes the data from this device immediately.

What is not claimed

  • Roxan has no security certifications or independent audits yet.
  • Messages are not end-to-end encrypted: the provider has to read the text to answer it.
  • Reminders fire only while Roxan is open in a tab.
  • There are no accounts yet, so access is by invite code, and history is not shared between devices.

Tell us about a problem

If something looks wrong, or you think you have found a weakness, write to us at oplocloud.com/contact.
