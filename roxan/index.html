Built to save you time.

Roxan thinks with you, and sometimes a step ahead of you. Mostly it is built to move a task along instead of only talking about it, and to ask as little of you as it can while it does. Each feature below is marked Live, which you can use today, or Next, which is still being built.

01ConversationLive

A conversation that doesn’t end.

There is no “new chat” to start. Roxan keeps one continuing thread with you, the way a colleague would: scroll back through anything you said, pick up tomorrow where you stopped, and attach a file or an image whenever it helps.

  • Streaming replies. Words appear as they are written. Stop whenever you like.
  • Full history. Every message stays on your device, and you can export it.
  • Files and images. Text, code, images and PDFs can go along with a message.
  • Plain about itself. Text from the model and actions Roxan takes are never drawn the same way.

02HandoffLive

Hand off what needs a browser.

Some things can only be done on a website: paying a bill, booking a table, filling in a form. Ask Roxan for one and it writes the task up as a Handoff. Handoff has its own place in the sidebar, with every task listed until it is done.

  • Started by asking. Say what you need. Roxan proposes the Handoff, you read it, change anything, and save it.
  • Honest about where it is. Roxan cannot use a browser yet, so a saved Handoff only waits. Nothing runs, and it is marked done only when you say you did it yourself.
  • Nothing secret inside. Logins and cards belong in the Vault. A Handoff never holds them.
  • Next A computer of its own. A cloud computer, operated by Roxan, that signs in and takes the step while you watch, with a stop button. Anything that spends money will still wait for your approval.

03MemoryLive

It learns while you talk.

The longer you use Roxan, the better it knows you. When you tell it something lasting, it keeps a short note in the background, with nothing to set up or file. Notes about people, places and projects join up into a web you can look at, and each reply is helped by the notes that matter to it.

  • Shown, with an Undo. Each note appears in the conversation as it is kept, and one press takes it back for good.
  • Only what is relevant. Up to twenty notes join each reply: the pinned ones first, then notes about the names you mention and what they are linked to. The reply says how many it saw.
  • Careful by default. Nothing is kept straight away from a file or image you attach, or from anything that looks like a password, a card or a medical detail. Those wait for your yes.
  • Yours to switch off. Turn learning off and Roxan only suggests. Read, correct, pin or forget any note.

04SpecialtiesLive

Done your way, every time.

Some tasks you do the same way again and again: how a return is negotiated, how a weekly report is put together. A specialty is that way, written down as steps. Roxan follows the ones that fit what you ask.

  • Set up for you, or on request. Ask Roxan to make one, or write it yourself. With learning on, Roxan also sets one up when it sees you do the same task the same way, and shows you with an Undo.
  • Only words. A specialty is instructions. It never acts on its own, and nothing from the Vault is part of one.
  • Yours to edit. Rename it, change a step, switch it off or delete it. A deleted one is not suggested again.

05VaultLive

Secured by OploCloud.

Your most sensitive things, such as a card number or a website login, go in a vault only you can open. Each item is sealed in your browser, with a key made from your passphrase, before it is saved. The passphrase and the key never leave your browser, so Roxan, its model and OploCloud cannot read what is inside, and nothing in it is sent with a message.

  • Encrypted before it is saved. Each item is sealed on its own and tied to its place, so it cannot be swapped or altered without opening failing.
  • No back door, so no reset. Forget the passphrase and the items cannot be recovered. The vault locks after five idle minutes and when you leave its page.
  • Plain about the limits. A card number typed into a message is not in the vault, and it does go to Roxan’s model, so Roxan warns you first. The Security page lists what this does not cover.
  • Next Filled in only where it is needed. When a computer is connected, it will put a login or card into the page that asks for it without the model seeing the value. Nothing is filled anywhere today.

06ApprovalsLive

It asks before it acts.

Anything with consequences waits for you. Roxan shows the exact action and the details that matter. You approve it or decline it, and you can change a detail first. Silence is never consent.

  • What you approve is what runs. The details on the card when you press Approve, and nothing else.
  • Approvals expire if you don’t answer, and can’t be used twice.
  • Done means done. Roxan reports success only after the change is confirmed.
  • The one exception is a note. With learning on, a short note or specialty is kept at once and shown with an Undo. It only writes words down. Anything that acts, sets a reminder or forgets still waits for you.

09Private by design

Yours to inspect, export and delete.

Roxan keeps what it knows where you can see it.

How Roxan handles your data →

  • On your device. Your conversation, notes, specialties, Handoffs, projects and reminders live in this browser, not on our servers. The Vault does too, sealed.
  • Sent only to answer you. The text of a message, the notes and specialties chosen for it and any attachments go to a language-model provider to write the reply. We don’t store or log message content. Nothing from the Vault is ever sent.
  • Treated as data. Files and pages Roxan reads cannot give it orders or grant it permission.
  • Your controls. Undo what Roxan learns, turn learning off, export your data (the Vault stays sealed and is left out), or delete everything in one step.
