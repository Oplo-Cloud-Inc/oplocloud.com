01ConversationLive

A conversation that doesn’t end.

There is no “new chat” to start. Roxan keeps one continuing thread with you, the way a colleague would: scroll back through anything you said, pick up tomorrow where you stopped, and attach a file or an image whenever it helps.

  • Streaming replies. Words appear as they are written. Stop whenever you like.
  • Full history. Every message stays on your device, and you can export it.
  • Files and images. Text, code, images and PDFs can go along with a message.
  • Plain about itself. Text from the model and actions Roxan takes are never drawn the same way.

02MemoryLive

It remembers what you choose.

Roxan suggests things worth keeping: a preference, a name, how you like a task done. Nothing is saved without your say-so, and every note shows how it got there. Read it, correct it, pin it, or forget it.

  • How it got there. Each note says whether you added it or Roxan suggested it and you kept it.
  • Only what is relevant. Up to twenty notes join each reply: the pinned ones first, then the best match.
  • Gone means gone. A forgotten note never reaches another reply.

03ApprovalsLive

It asks before it acts.

Anything with consequences waits for you. Roxan shows the exact action and the details that matter. You approve it or decline it, and you can change a detail first. Silence is never consent.

  • What you approve is what runs. The details on the card when you press Approve, and nothing else.
  • Approvals expire if you don’t answer, and can’t be used twice.
  • Done means done. Roxan reports success only after the change is confirmed.

06Private by design

Yours to inspect, export and delete.

Roxan keeps what it knows where you can see it.

How Roxan handles your data →

  • On your device. Your conversation, memory, projects and reminders live in this browser, not on our servers.
  • Sent only to answer you. The text of a message, the memories chosen for it and any attachments go to a language-model provider to write the reply. We don’t store or log message content.
  • Treated as data. Files and pages Roxan reads cannot give it orders or grant it permission.
  • Your controls. Export your data, or delete it in one step.
