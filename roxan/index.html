05Agents

Hand it the goal.
Roxan does the steps.

Roxan plans the work, uses the apps you connect, and sees it through — with every action logged, reversible, and yours to approve before anything is sent.

Roxan Spark is coming soon to Ultra, then Pro.4

And so much more.

Deep Research. Roxan reads hundreds of sources, reasons over what it finds, and hands you a cited report in minutes — with an audio version for the commute.

Long context. Give Roxan a 1,500-page filing, an hour of video, or an entire codebase, and ask about all of it at once.

Roxan Notebook. Upload your sources and turn them into study guides, briefings, and audio overviews you can ask follow-up questions of.

Personas. Save a custom Roxan with its own instructions and knowledge — a writing editor, a coding partner, an interview coach — and share it with anyone.

Bring your memory. Moving from another assistant? Import your preferences and past context in a few taps, and Roxan picks up where you left off.

Live translation. Talk naturally across more than 40 languages in OTeams calls, OMails threads, and face-to-face conversations.

Accessibility. Roxan describes your surroundings and on-screen content in rich detail, reads anything aloud, and lets you control your apps with your voice.

Roxan in OEdu. A study partner inside your courses — practice problems, step-by-step feedback, and explanations at your own pace.

Roxan in OMaps. Ask about places along your route — where to charge, what's open, what's worth the detour — without taking your eyes off the road.

Great power.
Kept private.

Roxan is designed to protect your privacy at every step. Roxan Nano runs on your device, so everyday requests never leave it. For bigger tasks, Oplo Private Compute handles your request on sealed servers — without storing it, or making it accessible to anyone, including Oplo.
