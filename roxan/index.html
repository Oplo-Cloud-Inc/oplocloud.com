01 — Roxan Assistant

Ask anything.
Get more done.

Roxan is powered by the Roxan 3 model family and is more helpful than ever.2 Richer answers, natural conversation, and a dedicated app that follows you from OSurf to your phone.

Rolling out in English. Usage limits may apply.3

02 — Live & Vision

Sees what you see.
On camera and on screen.

Roxan Live is built into every chat, so you can move between talking and typing without missing a beat. Point your camera or share your screen, and ask about what is in front of you.

Usage limits may apply.3

03 — Create

Imagine it.
Roxan makes it real.

Generate and edit images with Prism, direct video with Motion, and compose music — just by describing what you want.

Motion and music generation are included with Plus and above.5

04 — Everywhere you work

Built into every
Oplo app.

You don’t have to go to Roxan. It is already in ODocs, OSheets, OMails, OTeams, and OSurf — one keystroke away, and already holding the context of your work.

Roxan in the Oplo apps is included with Plus and above.

05 — Agents

Hand it the goal.
Roxan does the steps.

Roxan plans the work, uses the apps you connect, and sees it through — with every action logged, reversible, and yours to approve.

Roxan Spark is coming soon to Ultra, then Pro.4

And so much more.

Deep Research. Roxan reads hundreds of sources and hands you a cited report in minutes.

Long context. A 1,500-page filing, an hour of video, or a whole codebase — asked about all at once.

Roxan Notebook. Turn your sources into study guides, briefings, and audio overviews.

Personas. A custom Roxan with its own instructions — an editor, a tutor, a coach.

Bring your memory. Import your preferences and context from another assistant in a few taps.

Live translation. Talk naturally across more than 40 languages, in calls and in mail.

Accessibility. Rich descriptions of your surroundings and your screen, and anything read aloud.

Roxan in OEdu. A study partner inside your courses — practice, feedback, and explanations.

Roxan in OMaps. Ask about places along your route without taking your eyes off the road.

Great power.
Kept private.

Roxan is designed to protect your privacy at every step. Roxan Nano runs on your device, so everyday requests never leave it. For bigger tasks, Oplo Private Compute handles your request on sealed servers — without storing it, or making it accessible to anyone, including Oplo.

Oplo Private Compute

  • Your data is never stored
  • Used only for your request
  • Independently verifiable

  1. Roxan 3 is available in English at launch, with more languages rolling out through 2027. Some features are not available in all countries, regions, or languages.
