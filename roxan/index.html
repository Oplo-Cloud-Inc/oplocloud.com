Built to take work off you.

Roxan thinks with you, and keeps going after you stop. It holds your goals and their plans, runs routines on a schedule, watches pages for changes, and tells you only what is new. Each feature below is marked Live, which you can use today, or Next, which is still being built.

Always onLive

It keeps working when you leave.

Roxan runs on its own servers, not in your browser tab. Ask for a routine once and it happens at the time you chose, whether or not the page is open. What it found is waiting in your chat and on Today when you come back.

  • Three kinds. A reminder tells you something at a set time. A routine does what you wrote, on a schedule. A watch checks a page and acts when it changes. Run them once, daily on the days you pick, or every few hours.
  • It speaks first only when it is worth it. A message Roxan starts must be new and worth your attention. You choose how often it may, set quiet hours, and read why anything was held back in Activity.
  • It stops when something is wrong. A routine that fails three times in a row pauses itself and tells you. Run one now, pause it or remove it, and one button pauses everything Roxan does on its own. At most 30 background runs a day.

GoalsLive

A goal, and a plan toward it.

Say what you want done and Roxan keeps it as a goal with a short plan. It ticks steps off as they happen and shows what is next, on Today and on the goal itself.

  • Plan it together. Ask Roxan to break a goal into steps, or write them yourself. Add, remove or tick a step by hand at any time.
  • Ideas wait for you. Roxan can put an idea in a list for you. Taking one on turns it into a goal. It never acts on an idea by itself.
  • Yours to stop. Pause a goal, mark it done or drop it.

ApprovalsLive

It asks before it acts.

Roxan shows the exact action and waits for you whenever it would start something that lasts, keep something or remove something. You approve it or decline it. Silence is never consent. None of Roxan’s tools buys anything, sends email, signs in or runs a browser.

  • What you approve is what runs. The card shows the action; that action, and nothing else, happens.
  • Small things go through; bigger ones ask. Out of the box Roxan keeps goals, panels, ideas and reminders on its own, and asks before it starts a routine, forgets a note or saves a Handoff. In Access you set each one to allow, ask or off, and reading web pages can have a rule for a single website.
  • A page cannot give it orders. After Roxan reads a web page or a file you attached, it asks before it starts or changes a routine, keeps a note, saves a specialty or saves a Handoff, whatever your rules say.
  • It lapses, and works once. An unanswered approval expires after 7 days, and one approval allows one action.

MemoryLive

It learns while you talk.

When you tell Roxan something lasting, it keeps a short note and shows you, with nothing to file. Notes about people, places and projects join up into a web you can look at, and each reply is helped by the notes that matter to it.

  • Shown, with an Undo. Each note appears in the conversation as it is kept, and one press takes it back. A note you took back is not suggested again.
  • Only what is relevant. Up to forty notes go with each message: the pinned ones first, then notes about the names you mention and what those are linked to.
  • Careful by default. A note is kept straight away only if it rests on what you wrote yourself. One that comes from a web page or a file, or looks like a password, an ID number, or a medical or money detail, waits for your yes. A card number is never kept.
  • Yours to switch off. Turn learning off and Roxan only suggests. Read, correct, pin or forget any note.

PanelsLive

A place for what it makes.

When Roxan writes something worth keeping, such as a packing list, a plan for the week or a comparison, it goes in a panel instead of scrolling away in the chat. Open one to read it, tick it off or change it.

  • Three shapes. A document, a checklist or a table. A panel holds words and lists, and never runs anything.
  • Yours to edit. Change a panel by hand, tick items off, or delete it.
  • Next Small apps. Panels with a little behaviour of their own, built from a fixed set of safe parts, are not available yet.

SpecialtiesLive

Done your way, every time.

Some tasks you want done the same way again and again: how a return is negotiated, how a weekly report is put together. A specialty is that way, written down as steps. Roxan follows the ones that fit what you ask.

  • Made on request. Ask Roxan to make one, or write it yourself. With learning on it is saved at once; with learning off, Roxan asks first.
  • Only words. A specialty is instructions. It never acts on its own, and nothing from the Vault is part of one.
  • Yours to edit. Rename it, change a step or delete it.

HandoffLive

Hand off what needs a browser.

Some things can only be done on a website: paying a bill, booking a table, filling in a form. Ask Roxan for one and it writes the task up as a Handoff. Handoff has its own page, opened from Settings, with every task listed until it is done.

  • Started by asking. Say what you need. Roxan proposes the Handoff, you read it, and approving it saves it to the list.
  • Honest about where it is. Roxan cannot use a browser yet, so a saved Handoff only waits. Nothing runs, and it is marked done only when you say you did it yourself.
  • Nothing secret inside. Logins and cards belong in the Vault. A Handoff never holds them.
  • Next A computer of its own. A cloud computer, operated by Roxan, that signs in and takes the step while you watch, with a stop button. Anything that spends money will still wait for your approval.

VaultLive

Secured by OploCloud.

Your most sensitive things, such as a card number or a website login, go in a vault only you can open. Each item is sealed in your browser, with a key made from your passphrase, before it is saved. The passphrase and the key never leave your browser, so Roxan, its model and OploCloud cannot read what is inside, and nothing in it is sent with a message. The rest of what Roxan keeps is in your account on its servers; the Vault is the one part that is not.

  • Encrypted before it is saved. Each item is sealed on its own and tied to its place, so it cannot be swapped or altered without opening failing.
  • No back door, so no reset. Forget the passphrase and the items cannot be recovered. The vault locks after five idle minutes and when you leave its page.
  • Plain about the limits. Roxan refuses a message that looks like a card number, but anything else you type goes to its model and is kept in your chats, so put logins and cards here. The Security page lists what this does not cover.
  • Next Filled in only where it is needed. When a computer is connected, it will put a login or card into the page that asks for it without the model seeing the value. Nothing is filled anywhere today.

How Roxan handles your data

  • In your account, so it can work while you are away. Your chats, notes, goals, routines, panels, specialties, Handoffs, rules and the log are kept on Roxan’s servers. The Vault stays on your device, sealed.
  • Sent to the model to answer you. Each message, the notes and specialties chosen for it and any attachments go to a language-model provider to write the reply, and a background run sends what it needs, such as a routine’s instruction or a page’s text. Roxan does not write message text to its logs. Nothing from the Vault is ever sent.
  • Treated as data. Pages and files Roxan reads cannot give it orders or grant it permission.
  • Your controls. Undo what Roxan learns, turn learning off, set rules for what it may do, download everything, or delete everything in one step.
