Community.

Forums

Not open. A forum with four posts in it reads as an abandoned building, so it opens when there are enough people to fill it.

Open source

The parts of Oplo that are not the product get published as we go. The design system this site is built from is the first of them.

Office hours

A conversation with whoever wrote the part you are asking about. Ask, and we will find the hour.

Support.

There is no ticket queue to route you through and nobody in the middle. A developer question reaches the people who built the thing, and the answer comes back from them.

Not open, and we will say so plainly rather than take a name and a fee for a platform that has not shipped. When it opens it will carry the SDK, the tools and a way to put what you make in front of people — and not a day before all three exist.
