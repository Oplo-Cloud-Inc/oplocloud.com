Oplo Edu Learn

School that was built to be online.

Not a video call bolted to a homework folder. One room where the lesson, the work and the help all live.

What it does

Four tools that stopped being four tabs.

The room

A live lesson that opens in the browser with no install and no meeting id to paste. Video, a shared board, breakout groups and a hand to raise — and the teacher can see who has stopped following before anyone admits it.

Coursework

Set work once and send it to a class, a group or one person. Submissions come back in one place, grading carries a rubric, and the marks land in the record without anyone retyping them.

Mastery

Practice that adapts. Learners move through a skill tree at their own pace, and a topic stays shut until the one under it is genuinely understood — so a gap gets closed rather than carried.

A person, when it stops working

When the tracking sees somebody stuck, it offers a vetted tutor for a live one-to-one rather than another video. Booked inside the lesson, in the same window, at the moment it would help.

For teachers

The class is thirty. The attention is not.

In a room you can read a face. On a call you get a grid of muted squares and a feeling. The console watches the things a screen hides — who has stopped answering, who is guessing, who has been on the same step for eleven minutes — and tells the one person who can do something about it.
