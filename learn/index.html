New this term: families can follow grades, exams and the road to graduation in the family view.

Made for the way you learn.

Courses you work through by solving, practice that sticks, and one record that students, teachers and families all read the same way.

See OEdu in action.

Learning that’s a grade ahead.

OEdu brings courses, practice, marks and the record into one place — for the student doing the work, the teacher setting it, and the family following along.

Courses built
for solving.

Every unit is worked through rather than read past: questions before the lesson, a repair when an answer goes wrong, and one grade that counts.

Practice that
sticks.

Study sets drilled until they hold, and a tutor that offers a hint before it offers an answer.

A gradebook that
explains itself.

Missing, excused and late mean what they say, and every change to a mark is kept — what it was, who changed it, and why.

A record families
can read.

Grades, state exams and the road to a diploma in the family view — computed from the record, and never estimated to fill a gap.

From first lesson to graduation.
